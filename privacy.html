Privacy Policy

Política de Privacidade · Last updated / Atualizado em 2026-10-17

1. Who we are

HUB-Barch is a private tool developed and operated by João Vítor Barchfeld for the YouTube channel Barch GTA (the "Owner"). The Owner is the only user. Contact: joaovitorbarchfelld@gmail.com.

2. Data the app accesses

The app asks the Owner to sign in once with Google (OAuth) and receives access to the Owner's own channel only:

  • youtube.upload / youtube.force-ssl: upload and manage the Owner's own videos, thumbnails, localized titles and descriptions, playlists, and post comments from the Owner's channel.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read aggregate performance reports of the Owner's own channel (for example views by hour).
  • Video metadata created by the app itself: video id, title, scheduled time, privacy status.

The app does not collect personal data from viewers, subscribers or any other person, and it does not read other people's comments or channels.

3. How the data is used

Only to schedule and publish the Owner's own Shorts, confirm that they were published, and tune posting times. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the Owner's channel.

4. Storage and security

The OAuth refresh token is stored in a file with restricted permissions on a computer owned and controlled by the Owner. It is not uploaded to any third-party service. A local database keeps the status of each clip. Video files are read from the Owner's own Google Drive. Optional status/error alerts are sent to the Owner's private Telegram chat and may mention the name or status of a clip.

5. Sharing and transfer

YouTube API data is not sold, not shared and not transferred to any third party. It is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

HUB-Barch's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. YouTube API Services

HUB-Barch uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Retention, revocation and deletion

Data is kept only while the Owner uses the app. The Owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored token and database by deleting the app's local files. To request deletion of anything related to this app, write to joaovitorbarchfelld@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be posted on this page.
